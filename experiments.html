Drawing the background grid

Implementation notes for this site’s canvas background.

Deforming the grid

The grid is drawn as straight lines with points sampled along each one. Near the cursor, a point moves outward by an amount that depends on its distance from the cursor. Points outside that radius stay in place.

Drawing behind the page

The introduction handles pointer movement while the canvas sits behind the text. That keeps the text selectable. The grid settles when the pointer leaves, and the animation stops requesting frames once it has settled.
